Core product story
- Rig: each musician's endpoint for input, IEM mix, playback, talkback, patches, sheet music, and session tools.
- Cube: simple modular I/O path into GIG for fast setup without the full rack footprint.
- Racks: premium touchscreen-faced analog frames. The key new idea is historic input cards and historic output/print cards: physical analog cards that recreate capture and print identities from classic recording environments, then optionally hit the Tape Cylinder.
- Console: one command surface for rehearsal, studio recording, mix review, playback, and live operation. Lead with the 6-DOF SpaceMouse-style edit control.
- Venue: live playback and room translation layer. The important idea is mix-level reproduction: play the audio file at the recorded mix's level relationships, then translate that balance to the room.
